Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026. Contact: skaldandkreepyproductions@gmail.com.

Order and account information

Our services process account details, customer email addresses, order identifiers, purchased items, and activation records to provide access and support. Shopify processes store orders and checkout information under its own policies. Our application backend runs on Render. Information needed for fulfillment and account access is retained for operating those services; no fixed deletion period is promised.

SK Gmail Fulfillment and Google data

SK Gmail Fulfillment is an internal transactional email sender. The implemented integration uses Gmail's gmail.send permission to send activation and scheduling emails from the S&K mailbox. It exchanges the authorized mailbox's OAuth refresh token for an access token and submits the recipient address, subject, and message content to Google's Gmail API. This sending integration does not read or search Gmail inbox messages. Customers do not authorize their personal Gmail accounts.

OAuth credentials are configured on our backend and are not published in these pages. Fulfillment messages can contain customer email addresses, order identifiers, purchased product details, and single-use activation codes. Google handles messages through Gmail; Shopify supplies order information; Render hosts the sending backend.

Google user data is used for the described fulfillment function, not for advertising, sale, or training generalized AI models. S&K Productions' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Choices and support

For access, correction, or deletion requests, email our support address. We will assess what can be removed while maintaining purchase access, fraud controls, and any applicable record requirements. The mailbox owner can revoke app access through Google Account permissions; this stops automated Gmail delivery until reauthorized.

GitHub Pages hosts these public pages and may process request information under GitHub's privacy policy. These pages contain no added analytics scripts or contact forms. Product applications and the store have their own operational storage and account functions.
